# ECC release checks · 23 November 2026

## Constructions

We checked the dimensions and binary rank of all 22 submitted generator matrices, independently reconstructed their parity checks, checked their construction coordinates, and read both complete distance-verification records for every hit. All 18 released matrices have the rank and shape required by their target parameters. Thirteen use submitted generators directly. Four append a parity bit and one punctures the source generator. [Export instructions](../../skills/mathrsi-ecc/references/constructive-intervals.md)

For [72,43], the supplied checker and a separate exact parity-column calculation both excluded weights 1 through 10. A word with support at coordinates 0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 13, 14, 22, 25, 40 and 53 (zero-based) belongs to the row span of the released matrix. This proves its minimum distance is exactly 11. Parity extension gives an explicit [73,43,12] code. The independent calculation also excluded weights 1 through 11 for the released [74,43] matrix. For the other direct hits the distance guarantees use the two archived verification runs. ## Upper bounds

The package checker replayed all 37 steps for the 32 new corollaries, enumerated the codewords of eight lower-bound witnesses used by the upper-bound groups, and checked the interval counts against the archived snapshot. The 32 deductions import three published exclusions and 54 previous upper bounds. Nine meet an existing construction. [Step-by-step derivations](../../archive/intervals-57-20261123/3-corollaries-32/DERIVATIONS.md) · [Package checker and scope](../../archive/intervals-57-20261123/README.md)

For the proposed [41,12] exclusion, the release review repeated the independent catalogue check over 25,493 matrices. It compared 254,930 fields across implementations, all 125 pairs in unresolved equivalence buckets and 24,180 pairs in small exhaustive controls. For mother code 1980 we replayed all 6,912 stored rejection witnesses. Every one produced an actual word of weight 12. These checks cover the classification data and one rejection test. The full historical search over 20,709 mother codes was assessed from its source and recorded comparisons and was not rerun for v8. The archive omits the raw per-mother search outputs, the optional relaxed-five search variant and the second auxiliary witness file for mother code 2004. [Proof and handoff](../../archive/intervals-57-20261123/5-theorem-41-12/HANDOFF.md)

Gemini 4 Carbon reviewed the six supplementary technical questions against the submitted proof, sources and existing run records. It found no blocking mathematical issue under the two declared literature imports. One excludes [39,10,16]. The other counts optimal full-support [36,7,16], [37,8,16] and [38,9,16] codes up to equivalence. We independently read the latter paper. Section 2 specifies codes without zero coordinates, allows repeated columns and uses equivalence by coordinate permutation over the binary field. Tables A5 and A6 (p. 16) and A7 (p. 17) give respectively 8,170, 17,276 and 47 classes. The supplementary review and our checks are available for the source project's final decision on the two proposed optima. The release lists both as pending.
