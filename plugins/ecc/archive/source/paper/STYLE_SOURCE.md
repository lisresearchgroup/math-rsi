# Manuscript and style provenance

Official ICML 2026 style archive: https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/icml2026.zip (retrieved 2026-11-03). Style files are unmodified and retain their own notices. The paper uses the `preprint` option, so the front matter states a preprint rather than a conference appearance; the single document-level override sets the PDF subject to `Preprint`, superseding the style's proceedings string. This is a writing draft and does not assert ICML submission or acceptance.

Build: `tectonic --keep-logs main.tex` or `latexmk -pdf main.tex`.
