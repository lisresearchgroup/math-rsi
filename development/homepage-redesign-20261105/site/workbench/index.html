长度 43，为什么停在 16？Why does length 43 top out at distance 16?

可以把 10 位消息编码为 43 位码字，让任意两个码字至少相差 16 位。要知道第 17 位是否也能保证，需要两份证据。Ten message bits can be encoded into 43 bits so that every two codewords differ in at least 16 positions. Could a different code guarantee 17? That takes two pieces of evidence.

16 可以构造出来A construction attains 16

对一份具体的生成矩阵，逐一计算 1,023 个非零消息。最轻的码字重量为 16，其中一个来自消息 6。这给出可达到的下界。For one explicit generator matrix, all 1,023 nonzero messages were checked. Message 6 produces one of the lightest codewords, of weight 16. This proves the lower bound.

17 必须对所有构造排除Ruling out 17 takes every construction

假设存在这样的码。证明把它归到 172 类母码之一，每种扩张都需要三个两两兼容的顶点。逐类检验 619,053,789 对候选，连一条兼容边都没有。Any hypothetical code reduces to one of 172 parent classes. An extension needs three mutually compatible vertices. Across all classes, each of the 619,053,789 candidate pairs was rejected.
