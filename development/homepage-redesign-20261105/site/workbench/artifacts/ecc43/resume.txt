## 自己复核一条拒绝 / Check one rejection

[witness.json](witness.json)取自index169原始字节流的前8字节，按原词典序对应pair(0,1)…(0,8)。原F文件是33位压缩代表，按扫描输入的free坐标展开成40位a,b；u是原字节消息，ku由扫描矩阵原7行XOR得到。40位串左为坐标39、右为坐标0；7位消息右端位选择第0行。

[witness.json](witness.json) takes the first eight bytes of parent169’s original stream, corresponding to lexicographic pairs (0,1)…(0,8). The original 33-bit packed F representatives are expanded through the input’s free-coordinate list. The byte is message u; ku is the XOR of the selected seven original scan-generator rows. Strings are MSB first: coordinate39 at left and coordinate0 at right. The rightmost message bit selects generator row0.

逐位XOR a、b、ku，再数1，结果等于weight且小于16。这给出一个低权见证，足以拒绝该pair；无需证明它是最小权。原始生成行、pair序号、字节偏移和固定来源保存在JSON中。

XOR a, b and ku bit by bit, then count ones. The result equals weight and is below16, proving this pair incompatible without needing a minimum-weight claim. Original generator rows, pair indices, byte offsets and pinned sources are included in the JSON.

```js
const weight = [...e.a].reduce((n, bit, i) =>
  n + (Number(bit) ^ Number(e.b[i]) ^ Number(e.ku[i])), 0);
console.assert(weight === e.weight && weight < 16);
```
