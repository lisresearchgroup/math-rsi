# 全域覆盖 / Full-domain coverage

任意假想[43,10,≥17]经偶扩展为[44,10,≥18]，再由三列独立和对偶重量4结构，缩短到满支撑偶[40,7,18]。实际172份母码矩阵各有127个非零码字，共21,844次重量检查；图规范化核得172个不同坐标等价类。配合已发表“至多172类”，目录覆盖全部母码。

Any hypothetical [43,10,≥17] code extends to an even [44,10,≥18] code; three-column independence and a weight-4 dual structure yield a full-support even [40,7,18] shortening. The 172 actual parent matrices contribute 21,844 nonzero-codeword weight checks (127 each). Graph canonicalization distinguishes 172 coordinate-equivalence classes; the published upper bound of 172 classes makes the catalogue exhaustive.

E1 imports published nonexistence of [41,8,18]. The journal attribution is above; the theorem/page locator comes from Bouyukliev’s 2008 thesis, not the journal.

E2：满支撑[40,7,18]至多172个坐标等价类；Bouyukliev–Jaffe，Discrete Mathematics226,51–70 (2001)，DOI 10.1016/S0012-365X(00)00125-4。作者论文Table6.2（印刷158/PDF160页）列172。原作者历史分类计算作为进口。

E2 imports at most 172 coordinate-equivalence classes of full-support [40,7,18] codes. The author’s thesis Table6.2 lists 172. The original classification computation remains an external dependency.

令F_K为与K距离至少16的完整偶陪集代表集。标准尾部扩张迫使目标在同一个F_K中产生三个两两兼容顶点。对每个无序对(a,b)，实际消息u提供wt(a⊕b⊕uG_K)<16的拒绝见证，故图无边，更没有兼容三点。

Let F_K be the complete even coset representatives at distance at least 16 from K. A target extension requires three mutually compatible vertices in the same F_K. Each unordered pair (a,b) has an actual message u with wt(a⊕b⊕uG_K)<16, so the graph has no edge and therefore no such triple.
